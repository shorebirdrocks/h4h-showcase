Registration

Register for Hack4Her 2026

Registration for Hack4Her 2026 is now open!

Join us June 19-21, 2026 at VU Amsterdam at our 5th annual event, for an exciting weekend of coding, workshops, and collaboration.

Registration Process

The registration process is simple and straightforward. Here's what you need to know:

Registration Steps:

1

Complete the Registration Form

Fill out the basic information about yourself and your team.

2

Submit Your Application

Review your information and submit your registration.

3

Receive Confirmation

You'll receive a confirmation email with details about the next steps.

4

Select Your Preferences

Prior to the hackathon, you will receive separate forms to choose which workshops and challenge you're interested in.

Eligibility

Hack4Her is open to all students in the Netherlands. While all genders are welcome to participate in the event, only women are eligible to compete in the hackathon portion.

What You'll Need:

  • • Student ID or proof of student status
  • • Laptop and charger
  • • Enthusiasm and willingness to learn
  • • Optional: Team information if you are registering with a team
